Idea- framework description.
A) The poster header with logo and straplines:
1. the framework and its contents are described in full on the GitHub repository: 
https://github.com/hci-lab-um/heuristics-for-test-tool-design
2. idea-t was designed by Dr Isabel Evans, at University of Malta.
3. idea-t stands for “influencing the design-evaluation and acquisition of tools to support 
testing.
4. The tagline for the poster is “Better Questions. Better Decisions”
5. idea-t has 3 themes: Why? Who? And Context?
6. Each theme has a number of heuristics presented as questions, example activities that 
help answer those questions, relevant quality in use attrbutes, relevant product quality 
attributes, keywords, and alternative questions.
7. Theme Why has 1 heuristic, H01. Theme Who has 5 heuristics, H02 to H06. Theme 
Context has 6 heuristics, H07 to H12.
8. These are shown on the poster in a grid layout, bulleted below by theme. Sometimes an 
activity, attribute, or alternative applies to more than one heuristic so you will see repeats in 
the bulleted list below.

B) The poster content (themes are shown left to right and heuristics within themes left to 
right on the poster itself):
Theme 1. Why?
Heuristic: H01: why is this tool needed?
•	Activities: SWOT, Gap analysis, Stakeholder mapping, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Freedom from risk;
•	Product quality: user goals, appropriateness;
•	Keyword: Why?
•	Alternatives: Why else? Why not?

Theme 2 Who?
Heuristic: H02: who will use or be affected by this tool?
•	Activities: persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, stakeholder 
mapping, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Satisfaction;
•	Product quality: user goals, appropriateness, recognizability; 
•	Keyword: Who?
•	Alternatives: Who else? Who not?

Heuristic: H03: What previous experiences do people bring to this tool?
•	Activities: persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, training and support 
plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Satisfaction, Effectiveness;
•	Product quality: Learnability, appropriateness, recognizability, user error protection;
•	Keyword: experience?
•	Alternatives: What else? What not? 

Heuristic: H04: What communication needs or preferences do people have?
•	Activities: MS Inclusive design, persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, 
training and support plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Satisfaction, Effectiveness, Flexibility;
•	Product quality: Learnability, accessibility, recognizability, user error protection;
•	Keyword: communication?
•	Alternatives: How else? How not? Needs (accessibility) and Preferences (styles).;

Heuristic: H05: Do people want “tool mastery” or “task completion” if learning to use the 
tool?
•	Activities: MS Inclusive design, persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, 
training and support plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Satisfaction, Effectiveness, Flexibility;
•	Product quality: Learnability, accessibility, recognizability, user error protection;
•	Keyword: mastery or task?
•	Alternatives: How else? How not? Needs (accessibility) and Preferences (styles).

Heuristic: H06: What learning preferences do people have? 
•	Activities: MS Inclusive design, persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, 
training and support plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Satisfaction, Effectiveness, Flexibility;
•	Product quality: Learnability, user error protection;
•	Keyword: learning preference?
•	Alternatives: How else? How not? Needs (accessibility) and Preferences (styles).

Theme 3: Context?
Heuristic: H07: Where will the tool be used?
•	Activities: Persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, infrastructure and 
supported platforms plan, service modelling, ecosystem modelling, service 
blueprint, training and support plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Context coverage, effectiveness;
•	Product quality: Operability, appropriateness, user goals, maintainability, 
compatibility, functionality;
•	Keyword: Where?
•	Alternatives: Where else? Where not?

Heuristic: H08: What workflows is the tool part of?
•	Activities: Persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, infrastructure and 
supported platforms plan, service modelling, ecosystem modelling, service 
blueprint, training and support plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Context coverage, effectiveness;
•	Product quality: Operability, appropriateness, user goals, recognizability, 
maintainability, security, compatibility, functionality;
•	Keyword: Workflows?
•	Alternatives: Where else? Where not?

Heuristic: H09: What risks are associated with the workflows the tool is part of?
•	Activities: Persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, infrastructure and 
supported platforms plan, team shapes and autonomy, risk storming, service 
blueprint, training and support plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Context coverage, freedom from risk;
•	Product quality: operability, appropriateness, accessibility, maintainability, security, 
performance, reliability, scalability;
•	Keyword: Risk?
•	Alternatives: What else? What not?

Heuristic: H10: What autonomy of work styles is allowed in those workflows and teams?
•	Activities: Persona and archetype modelling, journey mapping, infrastructure and 
supported platforms plan, team shapes and autonomy, risk storming, training and 
support plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: context coverage, satisfaction, flexibility;
•	Product quality: operability, appropriateness, accessibility, maintainability;
•	Keyword: Autonomy?
•	Alternatives: How else? How not?

Heuristic: H11: When will the tool be used?
•	Activities: Journey mapping; infrastructure and supported platforms plan, 
maintenance plan, de-commissioning plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Context coverage, efficiency;
•	Product quality: operability, appropriateness, recognizability, maintainability, 
performance, compatibility, reliability;
•	Keyword: When?
•	Alternatives: When else? When not?

Heuristic: H12: How long will the tool be used?
•	Activities: Journey mapping; infrastructure and supported platforms plan, 
maintenance plan, de-commissioning plan, business case;
•	Quality in Use: Context coverage, freedom from risk;
•	Product quality: maintainability, portability, scalability;
•	Keyword: How Long?
•	Alternatives: When else? When not?
