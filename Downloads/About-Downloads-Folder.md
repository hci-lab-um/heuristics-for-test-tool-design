## About the Downloads area

In this area of the repository you can find idea-t artefacts to download

These include:

**Fast path lists and brief information about the heuristics**:
- [list of the 12 heuristics - pdf](idea-t-heuristics-list-v2.pdf)
- [list of the 12 heuristics - docx](idea-t-heuristics-list-v2.docx)
- [the heuristics as a meeting agenda - pdf](idea-t-heuristics-meeting-agenda-v2.pdf)
- [the heuristics as a meeting agenda - docx](idea-t-heuristics-meeting-agenda-v2.docx)

**Mindmap starter kits**:
- [xmind mindmap starter kit](idea-t-mindmap-skeleton.xmind)
- [idea-t mindmap starter kit as a bulleted list in Word document](idea-t-mindmap-bullet-list.docx)

**Workshop Materials**
- The [materials for the Workshop given at TestBash](../Downloads/TestBash-2025-Materials/AboutTestBashWorkshop.md) in October 2025 are available in a folder TestBash-2025-Materials, within this Downloads folder, and includes the PowerPoint, timetable, case studies, A3 poster mindmap skeleton, for record, but are out of date and will soon be replaced. 

**Poster (v2.0)**

*Print or presentation:* The [poster for idea-t v2.0](idea-t-v2-poster-A3=landscape.pptx) is currently available as a PowerPoint file where the poster side can be printed to A3 paper, or displayed as a presentation. 

*Alt text and accessibility:* In the PowerPoint file, the notes for the slide provide a description of the poster as a bulleted list (too long to put in alt text for the image). 
That description is also available as a separate [alt-text txt file](idea-t-poster-as-a-bulleted-list.txt). 

*Graphic:* A jpg `poster' of idea-t v2.0 can be downloaded from here [poster graphic v2.0](idea-t-v2-poster-A3-landscape-image.jpg))
